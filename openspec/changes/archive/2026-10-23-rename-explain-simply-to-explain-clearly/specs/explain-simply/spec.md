# Spec Delta

The `explain-simply` capability is retired by the hard rename to `explain-clearly`. No alias remains. Behavior is not ported unchanged: clarity is primary, the analogy is auxiliary, and delivery is a chosen form.

## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: explain-simply SHALL answer concept questions on two tracks

**Reason**: The skill is renamed `explain-simply` → `explain-clearly`, and the two tracks are no longer peers.
**Migration**: `explain-clearly` requirement "explain-clearly SHALL keep the rational track primary".

### Requirement: explain-simply evidence SHALL be authentic

**Reason**: The capability id moves to `explain-clearly`.
**Migration**: `explain-clearly` requirement "explain-clearly evidence SHALL be authentic".

### Requirement: explain-simply analogies SHALL preserve key constraints

**Reason**: The capability id moves to `explain-clearly`. The analogy remains, as an auxiliary section.
**Migration**: `explain-clearly` requirement "explain-clearly analogies SHALL preserve key constraints".

### Requirement: explain-simply SHALL use visual aids when they clarify

**Reason**: The capability id moves to `explain-clearly`. Visuals stay limited to concepts that have a shape, and plain text stays prose.
**Migration**: `explain-clearly` requirement "explain-clearly SHALL draw a visual only when the concept has a shape".

### Requirement: explain-simply SHALL not claim hands-on tutorial requests

**Reason**: The capability id moves to `explain-clearly`.
**Migration**: `explain-clearly` requirement "explain-clearly SHALL not claim hands-on tutorial requests".
