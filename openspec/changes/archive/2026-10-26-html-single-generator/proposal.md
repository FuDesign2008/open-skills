## Why

The review and evidence pages were hand-written HTML, so the dispatcher wrote both Markdown and HTML. That doubles the token cost and risks drift between the record and its view.

## What Changes

- All human-facing HTML is generated by the renderer.
- The dispatcher writes Markdown for records and small JSON for the review/evidence templates.
- `renderReviewPage` and `renderEvidencePage` generate those two pages from structured data.
- The Markdown remains canonical.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `goal-queue`: all human-facing HTML is generated by the renderer; the dispatcher never hand-writes it.

## Impact

- `skills/goal-driven-queue/scripts/render-html.mjs`, `render-html.test.mjs`, `SKILL.md`, `reference.md`, `scripts/human-facing-html-contract.test.mjs`.
- No dependency; no engine template edit.
