# Context

## 说明

The account of why a finished piece of work is the way it is: the problem, the cause, and the completed fix. A person reads it to understand the change.

## 证据留痕

The observed record of how that work was checked. It shows a screenshot when a check opened a screen, and the command record when it did not. A person reads it to see what happened. It does not carry the accept or return decision.

## 验收裁决

The person's decision that a finished card is accepted or returned. It is separate from the explanation and from the observed record.
